AND sometimes i find the loss overwhelming.

i grieve that my friends are moving away.

they're embarking on new adventures, excited to build their next chapters in cities far from here. i love and support them, and i'm excited for them too. but i will miss our walks through the park, our Sunday morning farmers market chats, our serendipitous run-ins on random evenings.

we will keep in touch for sure, but our relationships will change when we don't live in the same place anymore, when we don't have that shared context. when we won't feel the same rain from the same clouds. when we feel pressure to use our catch-up time wisely, sharing mostly the highlights and the Big Things. without these friends, San Francisco will be a little less warm, a little less friendly.
